Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026. Applies to Auto Text — Snippets & Templates, version 1.1.0.

What Auto Text handles

Auto Text processes text you enter or select to create and insert reusable snippets. Saved snippets and template answers can contain names, contact details, personal communications, or other information you choose to enter. The extension processes input events and text near the caret to detect shortcuts, show suggestions, and preserve editor content during insertion. It does not maintain a keystroke log.

The current website hostname is used for site libraries and exclusions. The current page URL and title are available to templates that use those variables. Auto Text does not maintain a browsing-history log.

Local storage and temporary data

Snippets, folders, tags, site libraries, and settings are saved in chrome.storage.local on your device. Auto Text keeps up to seven daily recovery backups within its storage budget and may take recovery snapshots before upgrades, imports, or restores.

Selected text captured through the context menu is temporarily held in session storage using a single-use token. Unused captures expire after five minutes. The text becomes part of your saved library only when you save the draft. Fill-in answers and clipboard reads are temporary and are not included in automatic backups unless you explicitly save the resulting text as snippet content.

Optional clipboard access

Clipboard reading is disabled by default. Auto Text requests clipboardRead permission only when you enable clipboard variables. Clipboard text is read when you request a template that uses it. The extension does not continuously monitor the clipboard.

Where information goes

Auto Text does not transmit your snippets, website text, browsing information, template answers, or clipboard contents to the developer or external servers. It includes no telemetry, advertising, tracking SDKs, or cloud sync. The developer cannot access your locally stored library.

When you insert text into a website or copy it to the clipboard, that text becomes available to the destination website or applications with clipboard access. Their handling of the text is governed by their own policies. Auto Text never automatically submits a form or sends a message.

Your controls and retention

You can edit or delete snippets, delete recovery backups, pause expansion globally, exclude sites, and disable clipboard variables. Deleting a snippet does not remove copies in existing backups; delete those backups separately. Export creates a JSON file on your device that you control. Exported files may contain sensitive snippet content; Auto Text does not encrypt them.

Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage from that browser profile. Files you exported remain until you delete them. Temporary fill-in values are discarded when the interaction or page ends. Browser/device security controls protect local access; Auto Text does not add its own encryption to stored snippets.

Limited use

Auto Text uses information only to provide its snippet and template functionality, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It does not sell or transfer user data to third parties, use it for unrelated purposes or personalized advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if Auto Text's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub Issues. Issues are public: do not include private snippets, clipboard text, or personal information.
